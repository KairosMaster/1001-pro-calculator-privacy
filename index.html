Privacy Policy — 1001 Pro Calculator

Effective 17 August 2026

Your calculations never leave your phone. Every figure you type, every answer, and everything you save is worked out and stored on the device. None of it is sent anywhere, because there is nowhere to send it — this app has no account, no sign-in, and no server of its own.

The free version shows ads, and ads collect data. They are served by Google AdMob, which collects and shares an advertising identifier, your IP address, how you interact with an ad, and basic diagnostics. This app does not choose which ads you see and never learns who you are — but that collection happens through this app, so it is set out in full below rather than disclaimed as somebody else's business.

Subscribing removes the ads entirely, and with them everything in the paragraph above.

What the app stores, and where

Everything the app remembers stays in its own private storage on your device. None of it is transmitted, and no other app can read it.

Saved calculations store the figures you entered, never the result — the answer is recomputed each time it is shown. That is a correctness decision rather than a privacy one, but it means the stored data is only ever what you typed.

None of this is personal information in the ordinary sense: it is calculator inputs and interface preferences. The app never asks for your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts or photos, and has no code that could read them.

Some of this may be included in Android's own backup to your Google Drive, if you have device backup switched on. That is a setting on your phone rather than something this app controls, and you can turn it off in Android's system settings.

Ads, and what Google collects

Ads appear only in the free version, and only on browsing screens — never on a screen showing you an answer.

To serve and measure them, the Google Mobile Ads SDK collects and shares:

  • Device and account identifiers, including the Android advertising ID and app set ID
  • IP address, which can be used to estimate a general location
  • App interactions — that an ad was shown, tapped or watched
  • Diagnostics — crash and performance information

This is used for advertising, measurement and fraud prevention, and is governed by Google's Privacy Policy. It is encrypted in transit.

You can limit it. You can reset or delete the advertising ID, or turn off ad personalisation entirely, in Android's Settings → Privacy → Ads. And subscribing removes the ads, and this collection, altogether.

Consent, in the UK and the EEA

If you are in the UK, the EEA or Switzerland, you are asked to make a choice before any personalised ad is shown, through Google's User Messaging Platform. You can change that answer at any time from the app's Privacy & disclaimer screen. Choosing not to consent does not restrict the app — you see non-personalised ads instead, and every calculator continues to work exactly as before.

The subscription

The Pro subscription is bought and managed entirely through Google Play. Payment details are handled by Google and never reach this app — it is told only whether an active subscription exists for your Play account, and nothing about who you are or how you paid.

Manage or cancel it in the Play Store at any time.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app deletes everything it stored. There is no server-side copy, because there is no server.

To remove data held by Google as an advertising provider, use the advertising controls in Android's settings, or the options in Google's Privacy Policy.

Sharing a result

The app can hand a calculation to Android's share sheet, as an image or as text, and it can copy a result to the clipboard. Both happen only when you tap the button.

When you share, you choose the destination app, and what you sent is then governed by that app's privacy policy rather than this one. This app does not choose a recipient, does not keep a copy, and does not write to shared storage.

Permissions

None of these is one Android asks you to approve — they are declared by the app and visible on its store listing. All of them arrive with the Google advertising and billing libraries; the calculators themselves need none.

What is not there matters more: no location, no camera, no microphone, no contacts, no photos, no storage access, and no permission to read anything about other apps on the device. There is no code in the app that could use them if they were granted.

The internet permission is never used to send a figure you typed or a result you calculated. Those do not leave the device — see the top of this policy.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and is not directed at children. It collects nothing itself, and asks for nothing that could identify a user of any age. Ads are served in accordance with Google's policies for general-audience apps.

Changes to this policy

If what the app collects ever changes, this policy is updated before that version is published, and any new permission is visible on the store listing.
