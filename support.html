The Pro subscription

Pro is one auto-renewing annual subscription. It is sold by the store you installed the app from, not by this app, and payment details never reach us.

To cancel or change it:

  • On iPhone or iPad: Settings → your name → Subscriptions.
  • On Android: Play Store → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions.

Access continues until the paid year ends. Restore a purchase from Get Pro in the app if you reinstall or change phones.

The 7-day free trial

The free trial is the store's own: seven days on the annual Pro subscription, started from the app and listed in your store account like any subscription. It opens all 1,001 calculators, the worksheets and every trade tool. Cancel before the seven days end and nothing is charged.

The trade tools

The trade tools are a single one-off purchase covering every trade. They are yours permanently, the purchase includes a year of Pro, and they are restored from the store on a reinstall or a new phone.
